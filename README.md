# Dimitrios Kasampalis

Earth observation and spatial finance. I turn satellite data into asset-level, audit-ready evidence on climate, nature, deforestation and carbon risk.

- Forester (Aristotle University of Thessaloniki), working with EO/GIS since 2014. Technical lead on three Horizon 2020 projects, now on ESA, World Bank and Horizon Europe work.
- Interests: physical climate and nature risk (TNFD, EUDR, CSRD), carbon and NbS verification, map accuracy and uncertainty, SAR, EO alternative data.
- Based in Thessaloniki, Greece. Open to remote contract work in the EU and Australia.
